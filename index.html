Controls

🖥 Desktop

On foot: WASD move · mouse look · Space jump · F use/interact · Left click tool · Right click (or R) grab/throw · X drop · 1-3 tools (hands/hammer/crowbar) · V camera · Tab map · Esc pause · P add passenger · M mute

Pilot: W/S pitch (W = nose down) · A/D roll · Q/E rudder · Shift/Ctrl throttle · G gear · Z flaps · B brakes · T autopilot · V chase/cockpit camera · F leave seat

📱 Phone

Left thumb: joystick (walk / pitch+roll) · Right side: drag to look · 💥 ACTION = use tool / spray · ✋ USE = interact · 🫳 GRAB = grab/throw · ⤒ JUMP (also parachute!) · 🔧 cycle tool · 🎥 camera · 🗺 map

Pilot: THR slider on the right, GEAR / FLAP / AP / BRK / ◀ ▶ rudder.

✈ Airports: land, slow down and taxi along the yellow lines to a free gate (HUD shows the distance). Stop on the gate's yellow stop bar to dock: the jet bridge connects and the front L1 door opens. Leave your seat, walk out the door, down the jet bridge into the terminal (departures board, café, check-in). Walk back down the bridge to reboard, take the controls and throttle up for pushback. T (AP) on the ground = auto-taxi (to a gate after landing, otherwise to the runway & takeoff). Sit in a passenger seat at the gate and the AI crew flies you to the next city.

Tips: Takeoff ≈ 130 kts with flaps 1. Approach ≈ 140-150 kts, flaps 2, gear down. Masks drop when the cabin depressurises — stand near one and press USE. Below 10,000 ft the air is breathable.
