Controls v3.5.1

🖥 Desktop

On foot: WASD move · mouse look · Space jump · F use/interact · Left click tool · Right click (or R) grab/throw · X drop · 1-3 tools (hands/hammer/crowbar) · V camera · Tab map · Esc pause · P add passenger · M mute

Pilot: W/S pitch (W = nose down) · A/D roll · Q/E rudder · Shift/Ctrl throttle · G gear · Z flaps · B brakes · T autopilot · V chase/cockpit camera · F leave seat · L AUTO LAND (when the banner shows) · G TAXI TO GATE (on the ground at an airport, slow or stopped — G again or any throttle/steering key stops it)

3.5: hold Shift to RUN on foot · Car: W/S gas & brake/reverse · A/D steer · Space handbrake · N horn · F exit (or click EXIT)

📱 Phone

Left thumb: joystick (walk / pitch+roll) · Right side: drag to look · 💥 ACTION = use tool / spray · ✋ USE = interact · 🫳 GRAB = grab/throw · ⤒ JUMP (also parachute!) · 🔧 cycle tool · 🎥 camera · 🗺 map

Pilot: THR slider on the right, GEAR / FLAP / AP / BRK / ◀ ▶ rudder. Near a runway a big orange 🛬 AUTO LAND button appears — tap it; move the stick to take back control. On the ground at an airport (slow or stopped) a big blue 🚕 TAXI TO GATE button appears — tap it and the plane taxis itself to a free gate; tap it again, move the stick or touch the throttle to stop.

3.5: 🏃 RUN button (tap = toggle, it turns green; or hold) · Car: joystick steers (push up = gas, down = brake/reverse) or use the big GAS / BRAKE pedals · 🚪 EXIT · 📯 HORN

✈ Airports: land, slow down and taxi along the yellow lines to a free gate (HUD shows the distance). Stop on the gate's yellow stop bar to dock: the jet bridge connects and the front L1 door opens. Leave your seat, walk out the door, down the jet bridge into the terminal (departures board, café, check-in). Walk back down the bridge to reboard, take the controls and throttle up for pushback. T (AP) on the ground = auto-taxi (to a gate after landing, otherwise to the runway & takeoff). Sit in a passenger seat at the gate and the AI crew flies you to the next city.

🚪 Places: every city has spots you can walk into (glowing gem + ring at the door). Walk into the ring and press F / USE (or tap the orange prompt). Eat to heal (or take food to go on a tray), play slots & arcade games for fun coins 🪙, buy souvenirs, snap 📸 photos (they count for the Tourist badge) and ride elevators 🛗 up to observation decks. MAP → Places lists them all with fast travel.

🛬 Auto-land (3.5): fly toward any runway roughly lined up (within ~65° and a wide approach cone, 1.5–14 km out, not too high). The AUTO LAND button shows the target runway, e.g. "LAX 25L". It flies the glide path, flares, touches down and brakes to a stop.

🚕 Taxi to gate (3.5.1): after an auto-land (or whenever you are stopped / rolling slowly on a runway, taxiway or apron) press G or tap TAXI TO GATE. The plane picks the nearest free gate, taxis off the runway, along the taxiways and up to the gate at real taxi speeds (≈20 kts straight, ≈8 kts in turns, walking pace at the gate), waits for apron vehicles crossing its path, and stops on the stop bar: the jet bridge connects and the L1 door opens. A dashed green path with arrows shows the way (also on the minimap). Any throttle, steering or brake input gives you back control.

🚗 Cars (3.5): every airport has a yellow-signed CAR RENTAL counter next to the terminal parking — rent a compact, sports car or SUV with fun coins. Or walk up to a parked / passing car and press STEAL… that earns wanted stars ★: police cruisers chase you. Get 120 m away from them for 10 s, hide indoors, or drive the car back into any rental lot and EXIT to clear it. If they box you in while you're stopped you're BUSTED (small coin fine, released at the airport police office).

🔒 Grok Jail (3.5.1): get busted and the cuffs come out: you land in the little GROK JAIL cell at the airport police office. 💰 PAY BAIL (18 🪙, key 1) to walk out now, ⏳ WAIT IT OUT (key 2, the timer always runs — about 36 s, your cellmate Grizz tells jokes), or 🥄 ESCAPE (key 3): tap DIG / press Space fast while the guard looks away — freeze when he turns 👀! Make it through and you're out free with 1 ★; get caught and +10 s. Never enough coins? Waiting always works.

🩺 Clinics (3.5): every airport has a CLINIC (red cross) beside the rental lot. Walk in, step up to a patient and press USE: pick what's wrong, pick the right tool, then drag it onto the glowing spot (or tap it). Coins + achievements!

Tips: Takeoff ≈ 130 kts with flaps 1. Approach ≈ 140-150 kts, flaps 2, gear down. Masks drop when the cabin depressurises — stand near one and press USE. Below 10,000 ft the air is breathable.
